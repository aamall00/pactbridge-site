InsureRemind

Never miss a motor-insurance renewal.

InsureRemind helps insurance agents keep every client's vehicle policy in one place. Upload a policy copy, and the details are read automatically and saved against the vehicle. Before the policy expires, the vehicle owner and the agent get reminders by WhatsApp and email.

  • Upload a policy PDF or photo; key details are filled in for you to review
  • Full policy history for each vehicle
  • Automatic reminders 30, 15, 7 and 1 day before expiry
  • Reports with Excel and PDF export
