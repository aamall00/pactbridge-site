How we work

From first call to the next renewal.

The same advisor handles your policy from the first conversation onward, so you never have to explain your history twice.

  1. Understand

    We learn what you own, who depends on you and what a bad year would cost.

  2. Compare

    Quotes from several insurers, set side by side on cover, not just on price.

  3. Place

    We handle the proposal, inspection and documents, and check the policy copy once it's issued.

  4. Claim support

    When something happens, we coordinate with the insurer and surveyor on your behalf.

  5. Renew

    Reminders go out well before expiry, so your cover and NCB never lapse.
